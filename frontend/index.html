About LandStack

LandStack is an integrated digital platform designed to bring important land-related information and services together in one place.

It connects spatial information, parcel identification, land records, verification information and planning-related data to make land governance easier to understand and access.

The platform is designed for both citizens and authorized users working with land information, providing a clear and organized digital interface for land services.
